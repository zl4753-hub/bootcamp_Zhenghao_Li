# Stakeholder Brief — Fed Rate Policy & USD/CNY FX Sensitivity

**Audience:** Chief Risk Officer / FX Treasury Team | **Cadence:** Monthly / Post-FOMC Meetings  
**Decision Supported:** Dynamic Currency Hedging & Foreign Exchange Exposure Management

## Context
Recent Federal Reserve policy shifts have heightened yield differentials between US Treasuries and Chinese Government Bonds, driving increased volatility in USD/CNY cross-border trades. Treasury risk teams need clear empirical estimates of exchange rate sensitivity to Fed rate decisions to protect operating margins.

## What You'll Receive
- **Sensitivity Analysis:** Econometric breakdown of USD/CNY elasticity relative to US-China interest rate differentials.
- **Scenario Analysis Tool:** Interactive dashboard evaluating FX volatility under 25 bps, 50 bps, and pause rate scenarios.
- **Monthly Briefing:** Concise summary highlighting structural regime shifts and policy intervention signals.

## Key Assumptions & Risk Factors
- **PBOC Interventions:** Central bank fixing rules can override market interest rate parity in the short term.
- **Data Latency:** Uses daily macro rate differentials and daily fixing rates.
